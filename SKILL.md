# /ig-viral

Reverse-engineer why a Reel works, then adapt the pattern. Never copy.

## Inputs
A Reel link, transcript or description, OR a niche for pattern discovery. Read `data/account.md`.

## Steps
1. Break the reference Reel into: hook type, first 3 seconds visual, structure, pacing, payoff, CTA, emotional driver (curiosity, status, fear of missing out, relief, surprise).
2. Name the repeatable pattern in one sentence (for example: "myth, evidence, flip").
3. Explain why it likely worked. Label this as an inference, since you cannot see private analytics.
4. Generate 5 original ideas that apply the pattern to the user's niche and angle. Each idea: working title, hook line, why it fits their audience.
5. Rank by effort vs. likely upside. Recommend one to produce first and hand off to `/ig-reel`.

## Rules
Adapt structure, never wording or footage. Do not claim a Reel "will" go viral. Cite only metrics the user supplied.
